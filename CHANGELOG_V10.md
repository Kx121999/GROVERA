# GOLDEN ROOTS V10 — Signature Commerce UX

## Implemented
- Premium visual mega menu with collection imagery.
- Guided 3-step Smart Product Finder (Theme Editor configurable).
- Search V3 with product + collection predictive results, popular terms and recent search history.
- Multi-variant Quick Buy from product cards through the real Shopify product endpoint.
- Collection sticky toolbar with compact/comfortable grid preference saved locally.
- Signature product purchase panel and inline functional B2B contact form.
- Cart “Save for later” moves products to the wishlist then removes the cart line.
- Cart upsell deliberately limited to two suggestions to reduce distraction.
- App-like mobile dock: Home / Collections / Search / Cart / Account (Wishlist fallback if accounts are disabled).
- Recently viewed section surfaced on the homepage for “continue shopping”.
- Legacy Shopify customer-account order history with one-tap reorder support.
- Premium empty-state, skeleton and micro-interaction styling.

## Important
The one-tap reorder theme template applies to Shopify legacy/classic customer accounts. Shopify New Customer Accounts are hosted by Shopify and do not use theme customer templates. Payment gateways, shipping rates and live catalog data remain Shopify Admin configuration.
