# GOLDEN ROOTS V18 — Cinematic Premium Store Experience

## What changed
- Cinematic full-bleed homepage hero with premium hierarchy and mobile treatment.
- Homepage rebuilt as a story flow: need → categories → curated products → farm workflow → offers → brands → recently viewed → confidence strip.
- Desktop category mega menu with visual category shortcuts.
- Search overlay redesigned with category routes and keyboard `/` shortcut.
- Product Cards V2 with cleaner hierarchy, hover detail layer and quieter secondary actions.
- Product page upgraded with premium purchase panel, key specs, refined gallery, service points and mobile sticky buy bar.
- Mobile navigation refined with cart badge and visual category shortcuts in the menu.
- Premium page heroes, cart/checkout surfaces, drawers, footer CTA, 404 and micro-interactions.
- Scroll progress, sticky-header state and intersection-based reveal motion with reduced-motion support.
- No Shopify, Meta or Google integration was added. V17 central catalog/cart/PWA behavior remains intact.

## Important
The checkout remains a storefront simulation. No payment is collected. Legal/business policy copy must still be approved before commercial launch.
