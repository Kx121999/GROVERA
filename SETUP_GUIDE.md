# GOLDEN ROOTS V9 — Setup Guide

## 1. Upload the theme
Upload `GOLDEN_ROOTS_Shopify_Theme_V9.zip` in Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes.

## 2. Create operational pages
Create a page with handle `wholesale` and assign template `page.wholesale`.
Create a page with handle `wishlist` and assign template `page.wishlist`.

## 3. Customer accounts
Enable Shopify Customer Accounts from Settings > Customer accounts. The theme links to the native Shopify account area.

## 4. Payment methods
Activate your actual payment gateway(s) from Settings > Payments. V9 automatically displays icons for enabled Shopify payment types.
If you enable Cash on Delivery manually, then enable the V9 COD note in Theme settings.

## 5. Shipping
Configure UAE shipping zones, rates and free-shipping rules in Settings > Shipping and delivery. The emirate selector in V9 is informational; actual availability/rates are determined by Shopify Checkout.

## 6. Reviews
Install a Shopify reviews app that supports App Blocks. Open the product template in Theme Editor and add the app block inside `GR Reviews V9`.

## 7. Product tags
Use `new`, `bestseller`, `preorder`, or `coming-soon` to control storefront badges. For a true preorder, Shopify inventory settings must allow the variant to continue selling when out of stock.

## 8. B2B requests
The wholesale template uses Shopify's native contact form. Test the form and confirm the store's contact email before launch.


### Final launch pass
After uploading V9, test checkout, shipping, taxes, customer accounts and all installed app embeds on the unpublished theme before publishing.
