# GROVERA Shopify Theme V3 — Launch Ready

## What is included
- Premium RTL/LTR storefront with GROVERA green/gold identity and the real GROVERA logo asset.
- Home: cinematic hero, 8 agricultural category cards, best sellers, brands, story, new arrivals, offers, trust strip, newsletter.
- Collections: collection hero, native Shopify filters, price filters, sorting, tags, mobile filter drawer, pagination.
- Products: gallery/thumbnails, zoom, variants, quantity, AJAX add to cart, dynamic checkout, WhatsApp, shipping/returns accordions, sticky add-to-cart, wishlist, recommendations, recently viewed.
- Search: full-screen search plus predictive product results.
- Cart: AJAX drawer with quantity controls, remove, subtotal and free-shipping progress threshold; full cart page + Shopify checkout.
- Wishlist: browser-local wishlist drawer, no app required.
- Mobile-first: 5-item bottom navigation, mobile menu, filter drawer and sticky product CTA.
- Pages: About, Contact, FAQ, Offers, Generic Page, Search, Cart, 404, All Collections.
- SEO: canonical, meta/OG tags, Organization structured data, Product structured data, favicon.
- Performance: responsive images, lazy loading on non-critical media, hero/logo preload, compact JS/CSS, no required third-party UI library.

## Upload
Upload `GROVERA_Shopify_Theme_V3_LAUNCH_READY.zip` as a new Shopify theme and preview it before publishing.

## Required store data before launch
1. Add real products, prices, inventory, variants and product images.
2. Create/organize collections and connect them to the homepage category blocks.
3. Build the Main Menu and footer menus.
4. Set GROVERA WhatsApp number in Theme settings > Contact.
5. Select Best Sellers and New Arrivals collections in Theme Editor.
6. Add real brand logos if needed.
7. Configure UAE shipping zones/rates, VAT/taxes, payments and optional COD in Shopify Admin.
8. Add privacy, returns, shipping and terms policies in Shopify Admin.
9. Connect domain and sender email.
10. Configure Meta / Google / TikTok tracking through Shopify Customer Events, official channel apps, or the approved tracking setup for the store. No fake tracking IDs are hard-coded in this theme.

## Product page content deliberately excluded
Per project direction, the theme does NOT include these custom blocks:
- لماذا هذا المنتج؟
- يستخدم في ماذا؟
- المحاصيل المناسبة
- المشكلة الزراعية
- مرحلة الاستخدام
- طريقة الاستخدام

The page stays focused on product title, gallery, price, variants, stock, description, specifications, shipping, returns, cart, checkout and WhatsApp.

## Notes
- Wishlist and recently viewed data use localStorage on the visitor device and do not require an app.
- Predictive search uses Shopify's product suggestion endpoint.
- Product recommendations use Shopify's recommendations section endpoint.
- Collection filters rely on Shopify storefront filtering being enabled/configured.
