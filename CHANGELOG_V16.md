# GOLDEN ROOTS V16 — Website Final Completion

## Scope
V16 finishes the standalone website experience before Shopify activation.

## Implemented
- Rebuilt all static pages on one consistent standalone shell.
- 26-item demo catalog across six agricultural categories with subcategories, SKUs, stock states and variants.
- Dynamic category, subcategory, search, offers and product pages driven by query parameters.
- Persistent standalone cart, wishlist, comparison, Quick Order and Farm Lists via localStorage.
- Complete cart and checkout UI without pretending live payment is active.
- B2B quote form, contact form, account dashboard, FAQ and policy-page shells.
- Arabic RTL / English LTR toggle from the same URLs.
- Mobile navigation, mobile dock, search modal, cart drawer and wishlist drawer.
- All category and product links now route to specific content instead of one repeated placeholder.
- No Google/Meta tracking added.

## Intentionally deferred
Live payment, shipping rates, live orders, customer authentication, email sending and real catalog data remain launch integrations.
