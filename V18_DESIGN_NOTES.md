# V18 Design Notes

V18 is a visual and interaction layer on top of the V17 Smart Storefront Engine.

## Direction
- Brand: deep forest green, restrained gold, warm paper whites.
- Product content stays dominant; decoration remains secondary.
- Desktop: editorial spacing, visual mega menu, cinematic hero.
- Mobile: native-app feeling, persistent navigation, sticky product purchase action.
- Motion: reveal and micro-interactions only; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Homepage Story
1. Cinematic brand promise
2. Shop by need
3. Agricultural categories
4. Curated products
5. Farm workflow / quick ordering
6. Current offers
7. Brands
8. Recently viewed
9. Confidence strip

## Data
All storefront product/category content still comes from `catalog.json` through the V17 catalog engine.
