# GOLDEN ROOTS V15 — Launch Blockers

The theme code is production-hardened, but the store should **not be considered ready to accept real orders** until these business settings are completed:

1. Add the real product catalog, prices, variants, SKU and inventory.
2. Configure UAE shipping profiles, rates and delivery rules in Shopify Admin.
3. Activate and test the real payment gateway(s); enable COD only if the business supports it.
4. Confirm UAE VAT/tax configuration with the business/accounting setup.
5. Add the real WhatsApp number, support email and official company contact details.
6. Replace/confirm Shipping, Returns, Privacy and Terms policies with company-approved legal text.
7. Create required pages and assign templates for Wholesale, Wishlist, Compare, Quick Order, Saved Lists and Brands where those features are enabled.
8. Create V11/V12/V13 metafield definitions actually used by the catalog.
9. If loyalty is wanted, connect a real loyalty data source/app. V15 deliberately hides unconfigured loyalty balances/tiers.
10. Run at least one real end-to-end test order on the live Shopify store: product → variant → cart → shipping → payment → confirmation.

Google/Meta tracking is intentionally outside this release, per project direction.
