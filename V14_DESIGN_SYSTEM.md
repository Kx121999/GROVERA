# GOLDEN ROOTS V14 Design System

## Direction
Premium agricultural commerce: calm, confident, clean and practical. Forest green provides authority, restrained gold is used only for emphasis, and warm off-white surfaces reduce visual fatigue.

## Core rules
- One primary CTA per decision area.
- Gold is an accent, not a full-surface color.
- Product photography gets more visual space than badges.
- Avoid decorative cards unless they improve navigation or trust.
- Mobile controls target comfortable tap sizes and preserve a persistent commerce path.
- Motion is subtle and disabled automatically for reduced-motion users.

## Theme Editor controls
Premium Design V14 includes global radius, density, motion, sticky header, soft surfaces, product-card quick actions, sticky desktop product panel, mobile dock and compact trustbar controls.
